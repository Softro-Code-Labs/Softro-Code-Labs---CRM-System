# Softro CRM user guide

Softro CRM is where Softro Code Labs keeps track of every client, every enquiry and every project we're trying to win. This guide explains how to use it day to day.

> **The golden rule:** if it's about a client and it isn't in the CRM, it didn't happen. Log enquiries, update stages and record outcomes as you go, so the whole team sees the same picture.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the CRM address your admin gave you (for example `https://crm.softrocodelabs.com`).
2. Enter your work email and password, then click **Sign in**.
3. You stay signed in for 7 days on that browser. To sign out, click the **arrow icon** at the top right.

**Your first sign-in:** your admin gives you a **temporary password** (it looks like `Softro-k7Mq-9xTe-Rw4p`). After signing in with it you'll be asked to **choose your own password** before you can use the CRM.

**Password rules:** at least 10 characters, with letters and numbers, and not containing your email name. A short sentence works well, e.g. *Galle sunset 2026*.

**Too many wrong passwords:** after 5 wrong tries your account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask an admin to unlock it.

**Forgot your password?** Ask an admin to **reset** it. You'll get a new temporary password and must choose a new one when you sign in.

**On a shared computer?** Always sign out when you're done.

## 4. Your daily routine

A good routine takes about 10 minutes:

1. **Open the Dashboard.** Check **My tasks for today** and **Leads waiting for a reply**. If the leads card is red, some leads are past our 2-working-day promise.
2. **Reply to waiting leads** (WhatsApp, call or email), then **log it** in the lead's Activity box. Logging a call, WhatsApp, email or meeting marks the lead as replied for you.
3. **Work through your tasks** and tick each one off. Schedule the next follow-up before you move on.
4. **Open Deals → My deals.** Move any deal whose situation has changed to the right stage. Look for cards with a **red close date** (overdue) or many days in the same stage.
5. **Log anything new** from yesterday: a WhatsApp enquiry, a referral, a new contact at a client.

## 5. Leads: new enquiries

A **lead** is someone who has asked about our services but hasn't agreed to a proposal yet. Every WhatsApp message, website form, referral or LinkedIn enquiry about a project should become a lead.

### Our reply promise: 2 working days

Our website promises a reply **within 2 working days**. The CRM tracks this for you:

- Every new lead gets a **reply-by deadline**: 2 working days later, Monday to Friday, Sri Lanka time. Enquiries made at the weekend count from Monday morning.
- The lead shows **Reply in 1 day** (grey), **Reply in 5 hours** (amber, less than a day left) or **Reply overdue** (red).
- The deadline stops counting as soon as someone clicks **Mark as replied**, **logs a call, WhatsApp, email or meeting** on the lead, or moves it to *Contacted* or *Qualified*.

> Public holidays and poya days aren't counted yet. Around holidays, reply sooner rather than later.

### Lead statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Nobody has replied yet |
| **Contacted** | We've replied and are talking to them |
| **Qualified** | It's a real project with a real need, and worth a proposal |
| **Unqualified** | Not a fit (budget, timing, not something we do, no response). Always give a reason |
| **Converted** | Turned into a deal. The lead is now read-only and links to its deal |

### Tabs on the Leads page

- **Open:** New, Contacted and Qualified leads. The default view.
- **Needs reply:** open leads nobody has replied to yet, most urgent first. The red number shows how many.
- **New / Contacted / Qualified / Converted / Unqualified / All:** one status at a time.

Use the **source** and **service** dropdowns and the search box to narrow the list.

### Add a lead

1. **Leads → New lead.**
2. Fill in:
   - **Title:** a short description of the project, e.g. *Booking system for a salon in Galle*.
   - **What do they want to build?** Pick the closest service.
   - **Source:** WhatsApp, Website form, Referral, LinkedIn and so on.
   - **Estimated value and currency:** a rough budget if you know it.
   - **Contact name, WhatsApp, phone, email:** at least **one** way to reach them is required.
   - **Preferred language:** English or Sinhala. Reply in their language.
   - **Business name / Existing company:** if we already have them as a company, link it.
   - **Their idea / requirements:** paste their message or notes from the call.
3. If you've **already replied** (e.g. you answered on WhatsApp straight away), tick **I've already replied to them**.
4. Click **Create lead**.

### Work a lead

On a lead's page:

- **WhatsApp:** opens WhatsApp with a greeting already typed, in Sinhala if that's their language. Local numbers like `077 123 4567` work automatically.
- **Mark as replied:** do this after your first reply. Stops the deadline.
- **Qualified:** they have a real need and want to hear how we'd build it.
- **Not a fit:** asks for a short reason, then marks it Unqualified. **Reopen lead** brings it back.
- **Edit:** change any detail, the owner or the status.

### Convert a lead to a deal

When a qualified lead wants a proposal or quote, convert it:

1. Click **Convert to deal**.
2. **Client company:** choose one:
   - **Existing company:** pick it from the list. The CRM suggests one if the business name matches.
   - **Create new company:** uses the business name they gave.
   - **No company:** for individuals.
3. Leave **Create a contact for …** ticked to copy their name, email, phone, WhatsApp and language into Contacts.
4. Check the **deal name, value, currency, service and starting stage** (usually *Discovery call*).
5. Click **Convert to deal**. You're taken to the new deal.

## 6. Deals: projects we're trying to win

A **deal** is a specific project we're working to win, with a value and a stage.

### Our pipeline stages

The stages follow how Softro works with clients:

| Stage | What it means | Move here when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery call** (20%) | We're learning about their idea, users and goals | A meeting or call about requirements is booked or done |
| **Proposal & quote** (50%) | We've shown how we'd build it and what it costs | The proposal or quote has been sent |
| **Design approval** (80%) | They're reviewing or approving screens before we code | They've agreed in principle and are approving designs |
| **Won** | Signed, paid deposit, or written go-ahead | Use **Mark won** |
| **Lost** | They said no or went elsewhere | Use **Mark lost** and give the reason |

The % is the typical chance of winning at that stage. It's used for the forecast.

### The deals board

**Deals** opens the board: one column per stage, one card per deal.

- **Move a deal:** drag its card to another column. On a phone, use the **stage dropdown** on the card.
- Each card shows the **value**, **service**, **owner**, **days in this stage** and **expected close date** (red if it has passed).
- Column headers show the **number of deals and total value** for that stage.
- At the top:
  - **Open pipeline:** total value of all open deals.
  - **Weighted forecast:** each deal's value × its stage %, a realistic estimate of what we'll win.
  - **Won this month:** deals marked won since the 1st, Sri Lanka time.
- **All deals / My deals:** switch between everyone's deals and only yours.
- **List** view shows deals as a table, including **Won** and **Lost** ones.

**Currencies:** deals can be in LKR, USD, EUR, GBP, AUD or AED. Totals are shown **per currency** (e.g. *LKR 850,000 + $1,200*). Different currencies are never added together.

### Add a deal directly

Usually deals come from converting a lead. To add one directly (e.g. a repeat client): **Deals → New deal**, or **New deal** on a company or contact page so the client is filled in for you.

### On a deal's page

- **Stage bar:** click a stage to move the deal.
- **Mark won:** closes the deal as won. If the company was a *Prospect*, it automatically becomes a **Customer**.
- **Mark lost:** asks for a reason (e.g. *went with a cheaper freelancer*). Reasons help us learn, so be honest and specific.
- **Reopen deal:** brings a won or lost deal back to the board.
- **Edit:** value, service, company, main contact, expected close date, owner, scope and notes.
- **Client card:** the company and main contact, with email, call and **WhatsApp** buttons.

## 7. Companies

Every business we deal with: clients, prospects, partners and suppliers.

| Type | Use for |
|---|---|
| **Prospect** | A business we're talking to but haven't worked for yet |
| **Customer** | We've done paid work for them (set automatically when a deal is won) |
| **Partner** | Agencies or freelancers we work with |
| **Vendor** | Suppliers we buy from (hosting, tools, etc.) |
| **Former customer** | Used to work with us, not currently |

- **Companies → New company** to add one. Only the name is required, but add the website, phone and city where you can.
- Use the **type tabs** and **search** on the list.
- A company's page shows its **contacts**, **deals** (with total won so far), **open leads** and details. Add a contact or deal straight from there.
- **Deleting a company** (managers and admins only) keeps its contacts and deals but unlinks them. Prefer changing the type to *Former customer* instead.

## 8. Contacts

The people we talk to at each company.

- **Contacts → New contact**, or **Add contact** on a company page.
- **Primary contact:** tick this for the main person at a company (shown with a ★). Only one per company; ticking it on someone else moves the star.
- **Preferred language:** English or Sinhala.
- **Mobile / WhatsApp:** used by the **WhatsApp** button.
- Search works on first and last name together (e.g. *nimal perera*), plus email, phone, job title and company.

## 9. Activities, tasks and notes

Every lead, deal, company and contact page has an **Activity** box. It's the history of everything we've said and done with that client, plus what's coming up.

### Add to the timeline

The box has three tabs:

| Tab | Use it for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Note** | Background or decisions the team should know | *Prefers to talk after 6 pm. Budget approved by their director.* |
| **Log a call, chat or meeting** | Something that **already happened**: a call, WhatsApp chat, email or meeting | *WhatsApp: sent the proposal PDF, they'll reply Friday* |
| **Schedule a task** | Something **to do later**, with a due date and time, an owner and a priority | *Call: follow up on the quote, tomorrow 10:00* |

- Scheduled tasks default to the **next working day at 10:00**. Change the date and time as needed (Sri Lanka time).
- You can assign a task to a colleague. It then shows in *their* task list.
- **High** priority tasks get an amber label.

### How it fits together

- Something logged on a **deal** also appears on that deal's **company** and **contact**, so a company page shows the whole relationship.
- A **deal** also shows everything from the **lead** it came from, so nothing is lost when you convert.
- On a **lead**, logging a call, WhatsApp, email or meeting counts as **our reply** and stops the 2-working-day clock.

### Upcoming and history

- **Upcoming:** open tasks, soonest first. Overdue ones are in **red**. Click the **circle** to tick one off.
- **History:** everything done and every note, newest first.
- To delete a note or activity, hover over it and click the **bin** icon. You can delete your own; managers and admins can delete anyone's.

### The Activities page

**Activities** in the sidebar is your to-do list across all clients:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Overdue** | Open tasks past their due time (red count) |
| **Today** | Due later today, plus open tasks with no date |
| **Upcoming** | Due tomorrow or later |
| **Done** | Completed in the last 30 days |

Switch **Mine / Everyone** to see the whole team's tasks, and filter by type (Call, WhatsApp, Email, Meeting, Task). Each item links back to its lead, deal, company or contact.

## 11. Enquiries from our website

The **Start your project** form on softrocodelabs.com sends each enquiry to **two** places:

1. An **email** to our inbox, as before.
2. A **new lead** in the CRM with source **Website form**, the service they picked, their language (from the English or Sinhala site) and their message. The reply-by deadline starts straight away.

You don't need to type website enquiries in by hand. Just reply, then **log your reply** in the lead's Activity box (or click **Mark as replied**). If the same person also messages you on WhatsApp, update the existing lead instead of making a second one.

## 13. Settings (admins and managers)

**Settings** appears in the sidebar for admins and managers.

### Team members (admins)

- **Add team member:** enter their name, work email, job title and role. The CRM creates a **temporary password** and shows it **once**. Click **Copy sign-in message** and send it to them privately (e.g. on WhatsApp). They'll set their own password on first sign-in.
- **Change role:** open the person, pick a new role and **Save changes**. They're signed out so the new permissions apply straight away.
- **Reset password:** gives them a new temporary password and signs them out everywhere. Use it when someone forgets their password.
- **Unlock sign-in:** shown when someone is locked out after too many wrong passwords.
- **Deactivate:** for people who leave. They're signed out and can't sign in, but everything they created stays. Reassign their open deals and tasks first. **Reactivate** brings them back.
- Status labels on the list: **Active**, **Temporary password** (hasn't signed in and changed it yet), **Locked** and **Deactivated**.
- Safety rules: you can't deactivate yourself or remove your own admin rights, and there must always be at least one active admin.

### Sales pipeline (admins and managers)

- **Rename** a stage or change its **win %**, then click **Save** on that row.
- Use the **arrows** to change the order of open stages.
- **Add stage** puts a new stage just before *Won*.
- **Remove** a stage with the bin icon. Only stages with **no deals** can be removed, so move their deals first.
- *Won* and *Lost* are built in. You can rename them, but they're always last, at 100% and 0%.

### Audit log (admins)

A read-only record of every sign-in and every create, update and delete: who, what and when (Sri Lanka time). Filter by type of record or by person. Website enquiries show as *Website / system*.

## 14. Good habits and data rules

- **One lead per enquiry, one company per business.** Search before adding so we don't get duplicates.
- **Write titles people can scan:** *Mobile app for a tuition class in Kandy* beats *New enquiry*.
- **Keep stages current.** An out-of-date board gives a wrong forecast.
- **Log every client conversation** (one line is enough), and **always schedule the next step** before you leave a lead or deal.
- **Always give reasons** for *Not a fit* and *Lost*. They show where we can improve.
- **Set yourself as owner** of the leads and deals you're handling, so *My deals* is accurate.
- **Client data is confidential.** Don't export, screenshot or share it outside Softro. Don't store passwords, bank or card details in notes.
- **Use real data only.** For testing, use the test site, not the live CRM.

## 15. Questions and problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "You don't have access to that page" | Your role doesn't allow it. Ask an admin if you need it. |
| I was suddenly signed out | Normal after your password was reset, your role changed, or you changed your password on another device. Sign in again. |
| "Too many wrong passwords" | Wait 15 minutes, or ask an admin to unlock you or reset your password. |
| A button I expect is missing | Same as above: it depends on your role. |
| A lead shows *Reply overdue* but we did reply | Open it and click **Mark as replied**. |
| I can't drag a deal | On phones, use the stage dropdown on the card. Won and lost deals must be reopened first. |
| The CRM is slow to open the first time in the morning | Normal on the test site: the database wakes up after being idle. |
| "Something went wrong" | Try again. If it keeps happening, send the admin the page address and the *Ref* code shown. |
| I entered something wrong | Click **Edit** and fix it. Only managers and admins can delete records. |
| I can't tick off a task | Only the person it's assigned to, the person who created it, or a manager can. |
| I need to change a task's date | For now, delete it and schedule a new one. Editing tasks is coming. |
